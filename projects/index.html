AI-Enhanced Guidance and Intelligence System

A personal AI assistant in the spirit of JARVIS: it sees, listens, remembers, and talks back. It's built privacy-first: the assistant, its memory, and its data run on hardware I control and are reachable only over my private network.

Capabilities
  • Wake-word voice commands with spoken replies
  • Camera vision, person tracking, and face recognition
  • Long-term memory of conversations and routines
  • Device security checks and self-diagnostics
  • Automated reports delivered over Signal
